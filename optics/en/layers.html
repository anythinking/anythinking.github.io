Layers of optical computing

Seven layers from light (L0) to industry (L6). Each layer is built from the one below — parts from effects, structures from parts, computers from structures. Click an item to see what it connects to above and below.

What to learn

A coordinate system that stacks optical computing into seven layers. If “where to go” is the map of institutions, this is the map of concepts — the same EO axis cuts across both.

Click a term to see its explanation and the chain from L0 to L6 — what comes from below (blue) and what leads upward (gold). If it gets crowded, pick “Neighbours only” above.

What this layer map does not cover

Details of nonlinear optics (a hidden layer between L0 and L1) · ONN training methods (really a third axis, so it does not fit in a layer) · foundry PDKs and tape-out practice · thermal stabilization and packaging.
