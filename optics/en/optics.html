Why nonlinear optics was missing from the 26-field map

It lies beneath every cell, so it never showed up as a cell of its own. ⑤ Nonlinear optics reaches 22 of the 26 fields even outside its own branch — the only zeros are gas sensing, photovoltaics, visual optics and optomechanics. The branches split, but the ground beneath them is continuous, which is why the three strata are needed separately.

The tree shows the main lineage, not the only ancestor — metalenses are ③+②, optical tweezers ⑦+①, OCT ①+②.
