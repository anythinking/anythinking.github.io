About

I studied electrical & electronic engineering at the University of Sydney, and I work as an engineer building superconducting magnets (NMR, up to ~20 T) — hands-on high-field hardware: REBCO tapes, joints, cryogenics, quench and current-injection measurement.

That hardware work feeds a broader interest — next-generation computing: optical accelerators for AI, and photonic / quantum approaches that reach past the limits of today's chips. I write to think, turning what I study (and where I get stuck) into the explanations I wish I'd had. Hands-on over hand-wavy: simulators I built, competition circuits I actually ran.

Looking toward graduate research in optical / photonic-quantum computing.
